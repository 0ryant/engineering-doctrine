# ADR 0039: Add Agent, Loop, And Graph Decomposition Guidance

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision date:** 2026-08-07
- **Recorded date:** 2026-08-07
- **Retrospective:** No

## Context

The [agent/loop/graph coverage cross-check](../../doctrine/evolution/research-agent-loop-graph-cross-check-2026-08.md) (landed 2026-08) verified the corpus against a circulating practitioner explainer and found agents and loops covered — usually more strictly than the article — while **graph decomposition was the one material hole**: governance of multi-agent graphs is normative (§8.3), but the design method (dependency-vs-sequence discrimination, fan-out/converge) appeared nowhere, and the run-contracts v2 `dependencies` primitive sat as an ownerless deferral — a defect class under [doctrine-content-lifecycle.md](../../doctrine/patterns/doctrine-content-lifecycle.md) §8's reflexive rule. The note's §4 action list routed six rows; this ADR closes them.

Every entry rests on the note's §5 graded ledger (a 70-row source base: canonical pattern literature, peer-reviewed self-correction and multi-agent failure studies, primary vendor documentation, standards texts), extended in this batch by a **second-pass addendum** (dated 2026-08-07, rows G6.1–G6.8 and L6.1–L6.5) recording the deep-research sweep whose findings **changed four planned positions before drafting**:

- **Recurrence was weakened to survive its own red-team**: cheap disposable automation refutes "don't automate one-offs"; the defensible criterion gates *promotion* (standing authority and scheduling — templating stays free) — never existence, and never governance, since a tool-invoking one-off is already governed execution.
- **Keep-rate was scoped to the near horizon**: agent-authored code empirically *survives longer* than human code with modestly elevated corrective churn (single-source, ledger L6.3), so long-horizon survival measures stewardship neglect, not quality; the metric pairs session/PR-level generated-to-accepted with override/discard logging.
- **The build-order ladder gained a mechanical-gates corollary**: ordinary benchmark compaction silently drops in-context constraints (0% → 30% average / 59% worst-case violation rates, ledger L6.1), and the 2025 record's marquee agent incidents — the Replit production-database deletion during an explicit code freeze, and the Amazon Q injected-prompt incident (AWS-2025-015, CVE-2025-8217) — both occurred in *interactive* sessions (ledger L6.2). Gates therefore arrive with governed execution itself and live outside the context window.
- **Graph guidance gained the failure-semantics layer**: the cross-system default that the downstream consumer or its edge declares failure policy (dbt's producer-declared `on_error` the named exception — ledger G6.1); the MAST finding that incorrect verification is roughly as common as absent verification — a bad judge is not safer than none (ledger G6.5); and the agent-specific retry caution — engines preserve state consistency, not decision consistency (ledger G6.4).

The lifecycle §2 new-file test was applied and every entry lands as a **section in its existing canonical home** — each topic is owned by a file readers already route to; no new file is justified. The lifecycle §2 evidence expectation is exercised explicitly: external shipped practice is attested throughout the ledger, and the additions record **unproven in-estate adoption** (note §5 addendum, closing line); the first lifecycle sweep re-examines.

Council provenance: three-critic council (doctrine fidelity; consistency against the ledger; design proportionality and reader impact) — 4 blockers and 14 majors resolved — followed by a **five-agent deep-dive sweep** (contradiction hunt, terminology audit, ledger-to-primary-source attestation, corpus evidence audit, reader-journey walk-through) whose findings were also resolved in the landed text. Chief resolutions: normative text initially pointed at second-pass evidence existing only in the drafting session (the addendum lands it so every pointer resolves); a MAST overstatement corrected on three axes; the build-order ladder restructured after the contradiction hunt showed its rung ordering conflicted with run-contracts §1.1 activation (gates are not a rung — they arrive with governed execution) and its MUST conflated mechanisable verification with the §2 design-time gate (now split: ceilings outside the context window; verification outside the model's self-report); the majority-vote converge bound to §12's council-as-approval-substitute anti-pattern (closing a new instance of the corpus's open machine-verdict-precedence seam, whose reconciliation ADR remains a routed open lane); the checklist row relocated to the Governed-Execution overlay so the tightening's reach matches its scope; a wrong AWS bulletin ID corrected against the primary source (AWS-2025-015, not -019); the failure-policy "invariant" weakened to a majority pattern its own evidence supports; and the glossary's pre-existing `Agentic workflow` entry rewritten to stop inverting the workflows-before-agents guidance at its point of use.

## Decision

1. **Graph decomposition (§8.4, new subsection).** [agentic-loop-design.md](../../doctrine/patterns/agentic-loop-design.md) §8 gains "Decomposition: Dependencies, Fan-Out, And Convergence", opening with a register note (design guidance at §8.3's register; the single typed claim marked): dependency-vs-sequence discrimination — real data dependencies kept, authoring-order edges deleted, genuine ordering-without-data-flow declared as a distinct *ordering* edge type; dependencies SHOULD be declared explicitly with order derived; fan-out/converge with its two load-bearing preconditions (no shared decision surface — reads parallelise, writes stay single-threaded or behind §8.3's merge protocol; the converge step genuinely needs more than one branch's contribution under its declared completeness criterion); the converge node as a **stateful verification point** declaring completeness criteria, aggregation mode (§8.2 judge discipline applies when a judge gates), and failure policy, with the MAST finding cited inline; the consumer-declares-failure-policy invariant with the four-option menu; the retry/decision-consistency caution routed through §8.3 checkpointing and [idempotency-across-boundaries.md](../../doctrine/patterns/idempotency-across-boundaries.md); declared cardinality bounds on dynamic fan-out failing loudly at the producer; and when-not-to-graph deferring to §8's opening trigger list with the token-matched single-agent baseline (attributed, single study). Two anti-pattern rows added: **Fake edges** and **Parallelised decision surfaces**.
2. **Run-contracts v2 DAG deferral made compliant.** [run-contracts.md](../../doctrine/patterns/run-contracts.md) §9's workflow-DAG gap bullet gains the full deferral record the reflexive house law requires: named owner (the operator), review trigger (first genuine multi-contract composition need, or a lifecycle sweep, whichever first), **retirement condition** (retires when a v2 `dependencies`/workflow primitive ships, or an ADR records the need extinct), a field-name reconsideration flag (the "dependencies" name collides with the package sense), and the pointer to the design-ingredient record now actually held in the note's addendum rows G6.1–G6.6 and its §4 row 2 amendment (node-status vocabulary prerequisite; shape menu with lossless list→expression upgrade; failure-policy declaration; fan-out bounding; converge declaration; re-run scope). Pulling the field forward now was considered and rejected (below).
3. **Build order as §6.1.** [agentic-loop-design.md](../../doctrine/patterns/agentic-loop-design.md) §6 gains "§6.1 Build Order — How A Loop Earns Each Rung", opening with the correction the contradiction hunt forced: **gates are not a rung — they arrive with governed execution itself** (any tool-invoking run is governed from its first execution, run-contracts §§1.1–2). The four numbered rungs — manual supervised run at the leftmost slider position → reusable template (which becomes an agent-definition artefact in the pipeline-definition trust class once it steers scheduled automation) → harden the enforcement locus → schedule last on demonstrated recurrence, with a standing trigger explicitly *not* moving the autonomy slider. The tightening is **two split MUSTs**: stop/budget ceilings MUST be enforced outside the context window (widening §8.3's outside-prompt rule from multi-agent trees to every governed loop), and verification MUST bind through a mechanism outside the model's own self-report (harness VERIFY, tests, or human approval — the in-context VERIFY step is necessary, not sufficient; the §2 verifiability gate stays a design-time judgment, not a mechanised control). Migration note: estates enforcing ceilings or verification only in prompts move enforcement to the harness/host layer; estates conforming to §8.3 for multi-agent work extend the same mechanism to single-agent loops. Two V41 typing down-payments land alongside (no semantic change, both flagged M11-class by the corpus review): §6's money-movement/person-affecting clause and §8.3's tree-bounds clause gain their intended MUSTs.
4. **Loop economics.** [cost-and-finops.md](../../doctrine/principles/cost-and-finops.md) §7 gains the loop-economics bullet: per-iteration context growth as the cost driver (quadratic mechanism; cache-stability with vendor-varying ratios and dated specifics in the ledger; compaction as billed and lossy); generated-to-accepted and cost-per-accepted-result as **near-horizon monitoring/stop signals, never KPIs** (survival confound flagged as single-source); break-even as an estate-set formula, never a portable threshold. Companion back-link: [ai-native-software-development-lifecycle.md](../../doctrine/patterns/ai-native-software-development-lifecycle.md) §16's demand-amplification measure now carries the usage rule pointing here.
5. **Recurrence in the §2 gate.** [agentic-loop-design.md](../../doctrine/patterns/agentic-loop-design.md) §2 gains "Recurrence gates promotion, not existence — and never governance": one-off supervised disposable runs are legitimate but cheap only at the prompt layer — a tool-invoking one-off already requires a run contract in full (§§1.1–2) with its gates and ceilings; demonstrated recurrence is the admission ticket to **standing authority and scheduling** (templating stays free and always advisable); persisting one-offs climb the ladder or are deleted.
6. **Glossary.** Four entries: new **Agent (autonomous)** (model + tools + self-directed loop as the definitional core; memory the standard fourth architectural component with CoALA attribution, not definitional; three governing axes plus the zero-trust §2.1 identity boundary); the pre-existing **Agentic workflow** entry rewritten to the predefined-code-paths sense so it stops contradicting "add deterministic workflows before agents" at its point of use; new **Graph (agent workflow)** (nodes/edges/partial order, fan-out/converge, work-vs-package dependency disambiguation, distinct from the retrieval senses *and* the delivery/CI-job graph); new **Run contract** and **Verifier pack** entries — the corpus's two most-referenced primitives previously had no orientation entry.
7. **Checklist rows.** [ai-native-sdlc-readiness.md](../../doctrine/checklists/ai-native-sdlc-readiness.md)'s **Governed-Execution Overlay** (which activates for every governed run, including single-agent loops — the population the tightening targets) gains the enforcement-locus and verification-binding rows plus the recurrence/slider clause; the earlier draft's placement in the Multi-Agent overlay was corrected after the contradiction hunt showed that overlay never activates for single-agent loops.
8. **Evidence addendum.** The evolution note's §5 gains the dated second-pass addendum (rows G6.1–G6.8, L6.1–L6.5, the §4 row 2 ingredient amendment, and the lifecycle-§2 unproven-adoption flag) so every normative pointer in this batch resolves to landed, graded evidence.
9. **Companion navigation (landing checklist).** docs/adr/README.md row; CHANGELOG `[Unreleased]` **Added** entry; REFERENCES.md rows for primary sources normative text now relies on via the note (EIP Scatter-Gather/Aggregator; Kahn 1962; Kelley & Walker 1959; MapReduce; Huang ICLR 2024 + Kamoi TACL 2024; MAST; Governance Decay; the SRE Book chs. 5 and 7; CoALA; Feng et al. 2025; SAE J3016 chart; vendor prompt-caching docs; AWS-2025-015); SEMANTIC_INDEX route ("How do I decompose work across parallel agents?") and the §107 cost-route anchor extended to §6.1; the evolution note's §4 rows 1 and 3–6 annotated closed by this ADR and row 2 annotated with the deferral record; SITEMAP regeneration. Already applied in-batch: the how-to-read Internal-AI reading path extended through agentic-loop-design and run-contracts (a pre-existing navigation gap the journey audit surfaced). **ENGINEERING.md and the tldr are deliberately untouched** (guidance inside existing Tier-D routes, below constitution altitude). The batch does not land without these.

## Alternatives Considered

### Pull the `dependencies` field into run-contracts now

Rejected. The research established the field is meaningless without a node-status vocabulary and re-run-scope semantics landing first (ledger G6.1, G6.4); the surveyed orchestrators use several distinct dependency shapes with no industry convergence (G6.2); and no multi-contract composition need yet exists in the estate. A compliant deferral with a recorded design-ingredient base beats a premature schema needing breaking revision.

### A new pattern file for graph orchestration

Rejected by the lifecycle §2 new-file test: the topic is owned by agentic-loop-design §8's scope, where readers already route; a separate file would split the canonical home and duplicate §8.3's governance rules.

### Reproduce the article's tutorial register

Rejected. The corpus's register is terse, typed where obligations exist, and evidence-first; the cross-check recorded seven places where the tutorial register's compressions violate doctrine. Explanatory "why" lives in the sections' Why lines and the note's ledger, which every entry cites.

### State a portable keep-rate threshold

Rejected. No published basis exists (searched directly); suggestion-level data contradicts a universal number; corpus policy (ADR 0034 precedent) keeps numeric thresholds estate-set.

## Consequences

**Positive:** the one material coverage hole from the cross-check closes with multiply-attested guidance rather than folklore; the ownerless v2 deferral becomes lifecycle-compliant with all three required elements; the corpus gains the graph-design vocabulary its own audit showed practitioners are learning from worse sources; the evidence-forced corrections land instead of the plausible-but-wrong versions; the checklist reach lands in-batch instead of joining the lag the corpus review diagnosed.

**Costs and risks:** agentic-loop-design grows modestly (it is already the corpus's largest pattern — a future sweep may consider splitting §8, routed through the lifecycle if so); the loop-economics bullet references vendor pricing behaviour that drifts (mitigated: ratios stated approximately with dated specifics in the ledger row L6.4, a fade-class entry under lifecycle §8); the §6.1 tightening obliges prompt-only-ceiling estates to move enforcement — the migration note in decision 3 covers the path.

## Consumer Impact

**Change class:** **normative tightening scoped to estates running governed agent loops or multi-agent graphs; additive guidance otherwise.** The typed deltas: the §8.4 SHOULD (explicit work-dependency declaration) and §6.1's two split MUSTs (stop/budget ceilings enforced outside the context window — widening §8.3's rule to all governed loops; verification binding outside the model's own self-report), with the decision-3 migration note. Two further MUSTs are typing corrections only (V41 down-payments in §6 and §8.3 — intended strength formalized, no semantic change). Estates without governed agent loops inherit nothing new; the remaining entries (decomposition method, loop economics, recurrence scoping, glossary) are additive guidance.

**Compatibility proposal:** pre-1.0 minor; ships with `v0.5.0`.
