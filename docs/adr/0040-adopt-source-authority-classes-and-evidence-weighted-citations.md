# ADR 0040: Adopt Source-Authority Classes And Evidence-Weighted Citations

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision date:** 2026-08-12
- **Recorded date:** 2026-08-12
- **Retrospective:** No

## Context

The corpus cites 771 external references across 154 domains ([census, council-reproduced exactly](../../doctrine/evolution/research-source-authority-and-evidence-weighting-2026-08.md) §2) with no taxonomy of what those citations are worth: NIST is the largest single dependency (93 references) with no watch on its withdrawal clock; EU law is cited 13:2 through unofficial mirrors; the academic tier is ~85% preprint; a tweet sits in a MUST-carrying pattern's reference list. The gap is live: the two research notes that grade sources each invented an ad-hoc four-class legend, and the [target-state council](../../doctrine/evolution/research-target-state-and-irreversible-decisions-2026-08.md) recorded as a finding in its own right that the legend could not express editor-reviewed magazines, statutory interpretive guidance, or authors' book sites. The promotion gate ([doctrine-content-lifecycle.md](../../doctrine/patterns/doctrine-content-lifecycle.md) §2) demands applied experience but has no source-authority dimension.

The [source-authority research note](../../doctrine/evolution/research-source-authority-and-evidence-weighting-2026-08.md) (three-critic council, unanimous ratify-with-fixes; census reproduced exactly; external spot-checks verbatim-clean) grounds the design: the two disciplines that grade evidence at scale — intelligence (Admiralty/STANAG 2511, ICD 203) and medicine (GRADE, OCEBM) — independently converged on **source class and claim support as separate axes**, with axis collapse the documented failure mode (87% of real Admiralty ratings fell on the diagonal); law, software engineering, and historiography supply the fields the design absorbs (scoped bindingness, appraisal checklists with vested-interest tests, role-relativity). Standards bodies supply the lifecycle mechanics ready-made: IETF's stability-gates-normativity with a registered downref exception path; ISO's dated/undated reference discipline ("for dated references, only the edition cited applies"; revision of a dated reference "shall be reviewed"); W3C's grading of a publisher's change-control regime; NIST's structured withdrawal tombstones with the DoD pin-of-a-withdrawn-revision divergence case; and the link-rot literature's verdict that durability is created at citation time or not at all.

Its own council supplied two design corrections this ADR carries: the **conformance/empirical claim split** (a claim citing a norm *as* the norm is satisfied by the pinned norm itself — corroboration is a test for empirical claims, not a demand for two copies of an RFC) and the **transcription/intermediary rule** (verbatim artifact retrieval keeps the underlying class; a single uncorroborated transcription takes the intermediary's).

Scope posture per [ADR 0027](0027-keep-public-doctrine-implementation-neutral.md): the pattern is portable guidance for any organisation maintaining citation-bearing normative doctrine; **this library is its first adopter and binds itself reflexively**, exactly as the content lifecycle does.

Council provenance: a three-critic implementation council (ADR fidelity; normative discipline and corpus consistency; red-team) returned unanimous land-with-fixes — 2 blocker reports (the same finding independently: the batch's own new citations violated the §6 metadata MUST at birth) and 12 majors, all resolved in the landed text. Chief resolutions: the pattern's References and the REFERENCES.md grounding table now carry class/pin/accessed metadata (the reflexive admission failure fixed at source rather than excepted); the diagonal-collapse-audit MUST gained its stated risk asymmetry (its C2 evidence base is flagged single-source in the text); §4 gained the norm's-own-class rule so conformance claims citing foundation-owned specifications (the corpus's largest bucket) are admissible; the sweep leg was bounded (exhaustive for MUST-supporting citations, sampled remainder) and the archival escalation wording unified to "where the estate operates archival tooling"; the promotion re-admission delta was typed once in its owner (lifecycle §2) and the harness's duplicate typed copy de-typed; X was ruled exception-proof; the register header now maps its columns to the normative-language §5 minimum record with behaviour-on-expiry; EX-0001's false claim about the target-state note's caveats was corrected and both prior notes' ledger legends annotated as retired; lifecycle §2's gate count, glossary alphabetisation, checklist anchors, and the CHANGELOG entry count were corrected.

## Decision

1. **New pattern: [source-authority-and-evidence-grading.md](../../doctrine/patterns/source-authority-and-evidence-grading.md)**. The lifecycle §2 new-file test is satisfied on both branches: the topic is durable and owned by no existing file's scope, and at minimum REFERENCES.md, the content-lifecycle pattern, the normative-language pattern, and the change harness cross-link it. Contents, from the note's §5 as council-corrected: the **S1–S7+X source-class ladder** with the hard-case assignment table (RC/draft ⇒ S3; guidance-vs-the-norm; preprint flag; foundation artifacts graded by their own review process; product docs mandatory-in-scope/persuasive-outside; marketing citable only as evidence of the vendor's claim; X = cannot-judge, distinct from low, never normative) and the transcription/intermediary rule; the **C1–C4 claim-support grades** with GRADE-style named modifiers and the written **independence test** (different organisations, different incentive structures — two posts by one vendor are one source); the **conformance/empirical split**; the **BCP-14 admission gate** — typed as: a new or promoted **MUST/MUST NOT SHALL be admitted only at C1, or at C2 with an explicitly stated risk asymmetry, or with a registered exception** (conformance MUSTs satisfied by their pinned norm; empirical claims about humans/organisations capped at C2 on practitioner sources alone); **SHOULD ⇒ ≥C2** with single-source flagged; **MAY/illustrative ⇒ ≥S6, pinned and dated**; and the **diagonal-collapse audit** as a named sweep check (suspicious class/support correlation = the axes have collapsed). Activation: estates maintaining citation-bearing normative doctrine; all other estates inherit nothing.
2. **Citation metadata and REFERENCES.md restructure.** The reference-entry shape from the note's §5: class, scope, role, durable identifier preferred as canonical leg (DOI/RFC number/dated TR URI), pinned-revision **or** rolling-rationale (rolling only under the ISO conditions — whole-document reference whose any future change the claim survives — and creating a standing monitoring duty), accessed-date, archived-url, optional content-hash, publisher-status, successor, deviation (pinned revision + publisher status + owner + **expiry** — the expiry element is this design's addition over the DoD precedent), supports-claims with strengths, review-trigger. REFERENCES.md gains the class declaration, the new-entry metadata shape, and the practice's grounding-sources table (its own rows carrying the metadata); **existing sections are re-bucketed cohort-by-cohort by the decision-5 sweeps, not in-batch** — a full class reorganisation would require grading the back-catalogue decision 5 forbids. Typed deltas (canonical owner: the pattern's §6): a newly admitted citation supporting a typed claim **MUST record its class, pin/rolling choice, and accessed-date at admission**, and **SHOULD capture an archived copy at admission** (MUST where the estate operates archival tooling — the tooling gap is a recorded watch, not a waiver of the principle that durability is created at citation time).
3. **Lifecycle wiring** ([doctrine-content-lifecycle.md](../../doctrine/patterns/doctrine-content-lifecycle.md)). §2's evidence expectation gains the source-authority dimension: **promotion to typed standing is re-admission at the higher floor** — supporting sources re-fetched and re-verified (content-drift check), re-graded, rolling references converted to pinned, publisher status checked (superseded ⇒ re-point or record a deviation), sub-floor exceptions called out in the promotion review. §8's sweep engines gain the **reference-status leg**: each sweep re-checks publisher status and link health for the cohort's citations (MUST, sweep-record evidenced), runs the diagonal-collapse audit, and handles **source events sweep-anchored** per the note's §6.3 table — rescinded/disavowed sources immediate on discovery; MUST-supporting events prioritised at the next sweep; calendar latency only where an estate names monitoring tooling and an owner. **Frozen history**: a Superseded doctrine file keeps its pinned citations untouched. **Composition boundaries**: ADR 0026 takes precedence for profile-carried sources (supersession fires the profile pattern's migration machinery; the citation record points at the profile entry); exception-register entries are [ADR 0028](0028-adopt-claim-level-authority-applicability-and-exceptions.md) exceptions carrying the normative-language §5 minimum record including expiry — reusable while in force, never permanent.
4. **Evidence-exception register: [EVIDENCE-EXCEPTIONS.md](../../doctrine/EVIDENCE-EXCEPTIONS.md)** (sibling to DEPRECATED.md). The downref-registry economics: claim, source, justification, approver, date, expiry — column-mapped to the normative-language §5 minimum record with behaviour-on-expiry stated; adjudicated once, reusable while in force, and standing as the evidence-upgrade worklist, swept with the register's other tables. **Inaugural entries land with the batch:** (a) the accepted-unrecoverable-evidence deviation covering both August research notes' ledgers (missing archived-url legs; recorded in the source-authority note's own caveats, with the target-state note covered by the entry's scope and annotated; owner: the operator; review trigger: the first lifecycle sweep once archival tooling is operable — absent at landing, the carryover explicit); (b) the Karpathy tweet in [agentic-loop-design.md](../../doctrine/patterns/agentic-loop-design.md) §References — registered with expiry and a pointer at the citation.
5. **Forward-only adoption; back-catalogue by sweep.** The gate and metadata rules bind **new and promoted claims from this ADR's landing**. The 771-citation back-catalogue is explicitly **not** retrofitted in-batch (the V41-scale trap); it routes through the §8 sweep engines cohort-by-cohort under the reflexive house law with all three elements recorded here: owner — the operator; review trigger — each release-coupled or standalone sweep processes its cohort's citations; retirement condition — the debt retires when every Active file's citations carry class and pin/rolling fields, or an ADR records a narrower steady state.
6. **Editorial companions.** Research-note ledger legend standardised in the change harness (notes grade sources with the pattern's classes plus verification state; existing notes' legends are annotated with a pointer, not rewritten — frozen history). EU-law mirror remediation across the four census-verified files (ai-adoption-controls.md ×8, REFERENCES.md ×3, privacy-and-data-governance.md ×1, ai-ml-systems.md ×1): EUR-Lex canonical identifiers (ELI/CELEX) become the canonical leg, mirrors demoted to convenience legs with the scripted-fetch limitation recorded as a tooling note in REFERENCES.md. Glossary entries: source class, claim-support grade, independence test, conformance claim, evidence exception (downref), link rot, content drift. [doctrine-change-checklist.md](../../doctrine/checklists/doctrine-change-checklist.md) rows: admission-time citation metadata; promotion-time re-verification; sweep reference-status leg.
7. **Companion navigation (landing checklist).** docs/adr/README.md row; CHANGELOG `[Unreleased]` Added entry; SEMANTIC_INDEX route repointed from the research note to the pattern as primary (the note becomes the evidence route); REFERENCES.md rows for the primary sources the pattern's normative text relies on (RFC 3967/8067, RFC 7322, ISO/IEC Directives Part 2, W3C normative-references and persistence policies, NIST withdrawal practice, GRADE, Garousi MLR guidelines, Zittrain and Klein link-rot studies); the research note's §7 rows annotated with this ADR's disposition; SITEMAP regeneration. ENGINEERING.md and the tldr untouched (authoring-process machinery, below constitution altitude). The batch does not land without these.

## Alternatives Considered

### Single-axis authority ladder (research Design A)

Rejected as the adopted core, retained as the migration vocabulary: one class per source with scope tags is trivially explainable, but reproduces the documented failure of every studied discipline — class becomes a proxy for support, a corroborated practitioner claim can never outrank an uncorroborated paper, and MUST-vs-MAY floors have nowhere to live. The census and note §2 already speak the ladder alone; the C-axis is what makes the gate expressible.

### Three-axis design with temporal validity as a grading axis (research Design C)

Absorbed, not adopted: pin/rolling and archival are **citation metadata fields** with reciprocal obligations, not a third grade. A third axis on every citation is overhead the note's own cost analysis rejects.

### Status quo (per-note ad-hoc legends)

Rejected by demonstration: two notes produced two incompatible legends, and the second's council recorded three canonical expert sources forced into "practitioner commentary" with statutory guidance and book sites having no cell at all.

### Adopt an external scheme wholesale (GRADE or the Admiralty code as-is)

Rejected: GRADE grades bodies of clinical evidence and needs trained raters (κ≈0.27 untrained); the Admiralty code grades raw intelligence reporting and its criteria (authenticity, competence) are undefined for engineering citations. Both contribute the design; neither survives domain transplant intact.

### Big-bang retrofit of the 771-citation back-catalogue

Rejected: the ADR 0032/V41 deferral history shows unowned retrofits compound and ownerless ones violate the reflexive house law; decision 5 gives the retrofit an owner, a per-sweep trigger, and a retirement condition instead.

## Consequences

**Positive:** the corpus gains one legend where it had per-note improvisation; MUSTs become mechanically auditable against their evidence; the NIST withdrawal clock, mirror-cited EU law, and link-rot exposure move from unknown to swept state; the promotion gate's "elegance is not evidence" gains its missing companion — a vendor blog is not an RFC; the exception register converts sub-floor evidence from silent debt into an owned worklist; both August notes' accepted archive deviation gets its discharge path.

**Costs and risks:** per-claim grading is real work with documented inter-rater noise (κ≈0.27 untrained) — mitigated by the written independence test, the conformance/empirical split removing the largest false-positive class, and forward-only scope; the two-axis scheme decays into a prestige ladder without audits — mitigated by the named diagonal-collapse check in every sweep; archival tooling is genuinely absent (perma.cc bot-blocked; Robust Links domain dead) — the SHOULD-with-watch in decision 2 is honest about this rather than typing an unmeetable MUST; the REFERENCES.md class declaration lands in-batch while full re-bucketing is sweep-routed (decision 5), so the corpus's largest navigation file is never big-banged; the sweep reference-status leg adds recurring labor — bounded by the exhaustive-for-MUST-supporting/sampled-remainder rule in lifecycle §8, with the sample recorded so under-sampling is visible; the pattern adds a fifth register-adjacent surface (EVIDENCE-EXCEPTIONS.md) — accepted, since the downref economics require a register and DEPRECATED.md's scope is content exits, not evidence floors.

## Consumer Impact

**Change class:** **normative tightening scoped to estates maintaining citation-bearing normative doctrine; additive guidance otherwise.** The typed deltas: the admission gate (MUST-floor with registered-exception path), admission-time citation metadata (MUST: class, pin/rolling, accessed-date; SHOULD: archive), promotion-as-re-admission, and the sweep reference-status leg. This library binds itself from landing; downstream consumers of the doctrine who do not maintain their own citation-bearing corpus inherit **nothing**. Estates that do adopt the pattern activate it forward-only, exactly as decision 5 scopes it here.

**Compatibility proposal:** pre-1.0 minor; ships with `v0.5.0`.
