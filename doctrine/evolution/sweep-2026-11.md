## Reference Status

Every external URL in active doctrine was requested once, with one retry: 483 unique URLs across `doctrine/` outside evolution notes, plus the root documents. ADRs and evolution notes are frozen records and were not rewritten.

- **441 returned directly.**
- **22 sit on lines carrying MUST, SHALL, or REQUIRED.** 21 returned directly. One publisher host (`genai.owasp.org`) denies automated requests.
- **35 were denied or answered without content:** 27 with HTTP 403 (`cisa.gov` 7, `genai.owasp.org` 6, `iso.org` 5, ACM hosts 5, and four single hosts), 7 with HTTP 202 (`eur-lex.europa.eu` 3, `data.europa.eu` 3, `sae.org` 1), and one transient HTTP 504.
- **One host did not resolve** from two networks at the time of the check (`europol.europa.eu`, one threat-assessment citation).
- **Six returned HTTP 404.** Three are repaired in this change: the GitLab Code Owners page moved, the Google Testing Blog address was mistyped, and the DFARS 252.204-7012 address had lost its trailing full stop in three places. Two pages are gone and are marked as such in [REFERENCES.md](../REFERENCES.md): a vendor feature-flag article cited only by the deprecated flag pattern, and a vendor specification guide cited nowhere else. One dead link sits only in the deprecated flag pattern and stays as frozen content.

Limits. A direct return shows the page answers, not that its content is unchanged. Content was read this month for the sources behind ADRs 0051 to 0059 (each research note records its reads), for the 21 fact corrections in the [full review](review-full-doctrine-2026-11.md), and for the standards and regulations in the [November source check](research-latest-developments-2026-11.md). Denied and empty responses are transport results, not verification.

Source events handled since `v0.5.0`: one citation withdrawn by its host was removed in the full review; the OWASP LLM Top 10 moved to its 2026 edition (ADR 0056); SLSA is restated from v1.2 (ADR 0053); one protocol-extension citation was corrected from a stable path to its beta path. No rescinded source was found supporting a normative claim.
