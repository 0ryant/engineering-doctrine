# Research: Bleeding-Edge AI Landscape Sweep And Doctrine Gap Audit (August 2026)

**Status:** research note (non-normative). **Date:** 2026-08-03. **Method:** 10-cluster landscape sweep + 4-area doctrine audit + adversarial verification. Proposals route via ADR per repo convention ([../../docs/adr/](../../docs/adr/)).

---

## 1. Purpose and method

This note records a full sweep of the AI engineering landscape as of early August 2026 and audits the doctrine corpus against it. The sweep covered **ten clusters**: governance (NIST/US federal guidance, OWASP GenAI/agentic security, international standards and regulation, frontier-lab safety governance), vendors (Microsoft/Azure/GitHub, AWS, Google Cloud), and cross-cutting (vendor-neutral techniques, agent interoperability/observability standards, AI-in-operations + AI supply-chain security).

The doctrine audit ran across **four areas**: (1) AI/ML systems, agentic patterns, RAG baseline, AI adoption controls, AI-native SDLC; (2) security SDLC, threat modeling, supply chain, API boundaries, privacy, configuration/secrets; (3) change approval, merge-path integrity, testing/verification, observability, reliability, incident operations, run contracts, verifier packs; (4) measurement/DORA, cost/FinOps, platform engineering, governance, glossary, references.

**Every candidate gap and stale claim was adversarially verified**: full-tree greps under multiple phrasings, direct reads of the cited sections, and live fetches of external sources. Findings that any doctrine file already covered substantively were **refuted and killed** (§5); several surviving findings carry verifier **corrections** (narrowed scope, fixed placement, or downgraded citations), which are used here in place of the original claims. Items the fetchers could not confirm against primary sources carry **(unverified)** flags throughout. This note is **non-normative**: it changes nothing by itself; normative changes go through ADRs.
