# Research: Source Authority And Evidence Weighting (August 2026)

**Status:** research note (non-normative). **Date:** 2026-08-12. **Method:** three passes. **Pass 1 (census):** every external URL in adopted doctrine (principles, patterns, checklists, tooling, glossary, REFERENCES.md, ENGINEERING.md, GOVERNANCE.md) extracted and bucketed by domain — 771 references across 154 domains. **Pass 2 (evidence):** two deep-research agents — (i) cross-discipline evidence-grading frameworks (intelligence, medicine, evidence-based software engineering, law, historiography), (ii) standards-body reference governance (IETF, ISO/IEC, W3C, NIST, link-rot literature) — each producing a graded ledger from primaries where reachable. **Pass 3 (synthesis):** the census, the frameworks, and the corpus's existing machinery composed into one proposed design and a lifecycle-intersection rule set. **Pass 4 (council ratification, same day):** three-critic council — attestation/dogfooding, census-reproduction, red-team — unanimous RATIFY-WITH-FIXES; the census reproduced exactly (771/154, every headline count), external spot-checks verbatim-clean, and the fixes (headline narrowed, gate arithmetic repaired via the conformance/empirical split, composition boundaries with ADR 0026/0028 stated, calendar SLAs replaced with sweep-anchored reviews, ledger self-grading corrected) are recorded after §8. **Source authority and limits:** §8 carries the load-bearing ledger with per-row verification state; single-source and transcription-verified rows are flagged. This note's own ledger is graded with the **proposed** scheme (§5), not another ad-hoc legend. Proposals route via ADR per repo convention ([../../docs/adr/](../../docs/adr/)).

## 1. Purpose and method

The corpus cites everything from IETF RFCs to a tweet, with no taxonomy of what those citations are worth. The gap is live, not theoretical: the two research notes that grade their sources each invented an ad-hoc four-class legend, and the council that ratified [research-target-state-and-irreversible-decisions-2026-08.md](research-target-state-and-irreversible-decisions-2026-08.md) recorded as **a finding in its own right**: three editor-reviewed magazine sources were forced into "practitioner commentary", and statutory interpretive guidance and authors' book sites had no cell at all. Meanwhile the promotion gate ([doctrine-content-lifecycle.md](../patterns/doctrine-content-lifecycle.md) §2) demands *applied experience* but has no companion rule for source authority: "Elegance is not evidence" has no "a vendor blog is not an RFC".

The operator's question decomposes into four: what do we currently cite (§2); how do mature disciplines grade sources and evidence (§3); how do standards bodies wire references into content lifecycle (§4); and what should this library adopt for additions, promotions, demotions, and updates (§§5–6).

Headline: **no discipline that has stress-tested evidence grading for decades lets source rank settle claim support — and the two that grade at scale, intelligence and medicine, independently landed on the same two-axis design, with axis collapse the canonical failure mode.** The other three literatures do not supply further instances of the two-axis core; they supply the fields the design absorbs — scoped bindingness (law), appraisal checklists with vested-interest tests (software engineering), role-relativity (historiography). The standards bodies supply the lifecycle half ready-made: stability gates normativity (IETF), undated references delegate future change control while dated ones create a review duty on every revision (ISO), publishers' change-control regimes are themselves gradeable (W3C), and withdrawal is loud, structured, and survivable by downstream pins only as an explicit recorded deviation (NIST × DoD). The census shows the corpus needs all of it: its single biggest dependency is NIST (~93 references), its academic tier is 85% preprint, its EU-law citations run 13:2 through unofficial mirrors, and one adopted pattern cites a tweet.

## 3. What the disciplines converge on

Condensed from the frameworks ledger (§8 rows F1–F15). Five literatures: the two that grade evidence at scale converge on the two-axis core; the other three contribute the orthogonal fields §5 absorbs:

1. **Intelligence (Admiralty/STANAG 2511; ICD 203).** The purest two-axis system: source reliability (A–F) × information credibility (1–6), graded independently — an A-source can deliver a doubtful item; an F-source a confirmed one. Credibility 1 is defined by **independent corroboration**, with independence of provenance the operative test. Both axes carry an explicit **"cannot judge"** value distinct from "low". ICD 203 adds a second separation: *likelihood of the event* vs *confidence in the judgment*, forbidden from sharing a sentence. Documented failure: **87% of real ratings collapsed onto the A1/B2/C3 diagonal** [F3, single-source figure] — without audits, the second axis decays into ritual.
2. **Medicine (GRADE; Oxford CEBM).** GRADE grades **certainty per claim, not per citation**: evidence starts at a level set by source type, then moves on named modifiers (risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias down; large effect, dose-response, confounding-works-against up). Separately, **recommendation strength is decoupled from evidence certainty** — a strong recommendation can rest on moderate evidence when the risk asymmetry is severe, *stated explicitly*. CEBM 2011 made the hierarchy **question-type-relative**: the best source depends on what kind of claim is being supported. The pyramid critique [F6]: design hierarchies used as rank-without-appraisal fail — "credible systematic review can summarize biased evidence and poorly done systematic reviews can summarize well done trials".
3. **Evidence-based SE (Kitchenham/Dybå; Garousi's multivocal guidelines).** SE never adopted a native design hierarchy — checklists over ranks. The grey-literature guidelines grade practitioner sources on **two continuous dimensions — author expertise × outlet control** (martinfowler.com is high-expertise/low-control; a news site the reverse) — with tier membership *content-mobile* and a per-source checklist including **vested interest**. The decisive datum for a citation-bearing library: **49% of grey-literature URLs in surveyed SE research were already dead** [F15, single-source].
4. **Law.** Grades **bindingness within a scope**, not truth: mandatory only in jurisdiction; everything else persuasive by degree; secondary sources always persuasive. Transplants almost verbatim: an RFC is "mandatory" for claims about that protocol; AWS docs for claims about AWS behaviour; both merely persuasive for "how to build systems" — so authority class must carry a **scope field**, and out-of-scope citation silently demotes. Second lesson (Scalia on Restatements [F12]): when an interpretive body stops describing and starts aspiring, its weight for is-claims collapses though its prestige is unchanged — grade the artifact's stance, not the organisation's brand.
5. **Historiography.** Classification is **role-relative to the question**: the same vendor postmortem is primary for "what happened in that outage", secondary for "retries need jitter", marketing for "our product prevents outages". Class lives on the *(source, claim)* pair — a fixed label per domain name is a category error (the census's github.com row is this problem at scale).

## 4. How standards bodies wire references into lifecycle

Condensed from the standards-mechanics ledger (§8 rows M1–M15). The one-line crib per body:

1. **IETF — stability gates normativity; exceptions are registered once, in public.** Normative = "must be read to understand or implement"; standards-track documents must not normatively depend on lower-maturity work — except via the **downref** path (RFC 3967/8067): the exception is explicitly called out in review, then recorded in a **registry so it is adjudicated once and reused**. No normative references to work-in-progress — publication *blocks* on reference stability. RFCs are immutable; supersession travels as bidirectional Obsoletes/Obsoleted-By metadata; where a dated URI exists, "its use is required" (RFC 7322).
2. **ISO/IEC Directives Part 2 — the dated/undated rule is the updates policy.** "For dated references, only the edition cited applies. For undated references, the latest edition… applies." Undated is permitted **only** when the citing document can absorb **all future changes** of the referenced one — an undated reference is a deliberate delegation of change control. Dated creates the reciprocal duty: on amendment or revision of the referenced document, dated references "**shall be reviewed** to assess whether they shall be updated". Sub-element citations (clause/table/control-ID) are **always dated** — renumbering breaks them silently. Normative references to external bodies require authority, public availability, FRAND access, and the publisher's **agreement to notify of revisions**; revising your own document revalidates every reference.
3. **W3C — grade the publisher's change-control regime, not just the text.** Normative references (especially external or living-standard) screen on stability claims, explicit change-control policy, implementation experience, and recovery strategy for incompatible change. Every TR has a **dated URI (persistent forever) and a rolling URI** — the pinned/rolling choice is offered to citers as first-class infrastructure. Terminal statuses are distinct: superseded-by-successor ≠ obsolete-no-longer-recommended ≠ rescinded-disavowed, and each demands different downstream action.
4. **NIST — retire loudly; expect downstream pins to outlive supersession.** Withdrawn publications keep their URL/DOI and gain a structured tombstone *on the artifact itself* (withdrawal date, note, superseding publication with resolvable identifier). The live divergence case: NIST withdrew SP 800-171 Rev. 2 in May 2024, while a DoD class deviation deliberately **pinned an entire contractor ecosystem to the withdrawn revision** — a legitimate move *because it is an explicit, owned deviation record*, not drift (the DoD deviation runs until rescinded; the **expiry** element in §5's `deviation` field is this design's addition over the precedent, aligning it with the exception contract). Publisher status and downstream pin can diverge; the divergence is itself reviewable state.
5. **Link-rot literature — durability is created at citation time, never later.** ~70% of Harvard Law Review URLs and ~50% of Supreme Court-opinion URLs no longer reach the cited content [M13]; one in five STM articles suffers reference rot, and only ~27% of cited URLs had a near-publication archived snapshot [M14]. The failure splits into **link rot** (URL dies) and **content drift** (URL lives, content silently changes) — drift is the invisible one. During pass-2 research itself, four of ~25 canonical governance URLs were degraded (a spec domain failing DNS, two bodies bot-blocking, one moved) — the phenomenon demonstrated itself in an afternoon.

## 5. Proposed design

**Two axes plus citation metadata** — the convergent design of Admiralty × GRADE, with the legal scope rule and the ISO temporal discipline absorbed as fields:

**Axis 1 — source class, on each citation** (with `scope`, `role`, and a temporal field):

| Class | Definition | Census exemplars | Hard-case rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **S1** | The norm itself, from its owner, ratified form | RFC, ISO edition, EUR-Lex text | RC/draft ⇒ S3; mirror URLs are convenience legs only |
| **S2** | Statutory-body / institutional guidance with a mandate over the domain | NIST SP, CISA/NCSC, **EDPB guidelines** | Interpretive, not the norm — the guidance-vs-law split the old legend lacked |
| **S3** | Peer-reviewed research; standards-in-progress (flagged provisional) | ACM/IEEE venues; **arXiv with `preprint: true`** | Preprints do not carry peer-review weight |
| **S4** | Edited/reviewed artifacts: books, editor-reviewed magazines, foundation specs *graded by the artifact's own review process*, disclosed-method research programs | SLSA, ASVS, SRE books, **IEEE Software/HBR columns**, DORA reports | The magazine tier the old legend forced into "practitioner"; OWASP Top 10 = awareness ⇒ risk-salience claims only; undisclosed-method consultancy ⇒ S6/S7 |
| **S5** | Product-scoped primary documentation | AWS/MS/GitHub docs, MCP spec | **Mandatory within product scope, persuasive outside it** — out-of-scope citation silently demotes |
| **S6** | Named-expert practitioner; vendor engineering | martinfowler.com, vendor postmortems | `role=primary` for the vendor's own incidents; `role=secondary` for generalised advice |
| **S7** | Community/UGC, marketing, press | tweets, marketing pages | Marketing citable only as evidence *of the vendor's claim*, never of its truth |
| **X** | Cannot judge (anonymous, unattributable) | orphaned gists | "Unknown" is a distinct state from "low"; never supports normative claims |

**Axis 2 — claim support, on each material doctrine claim** (GRADE-style, computed from the citation set): **C1 corroborated** (≥2 *independent* in-scope S1–S4 — different organisations, different incentive structures; two posts by one vendor are one source), **C2 probable** (one in-scope S1–S4, or ≥2 independent S5–S6), **C3 plausible** (single S5–S6 or practitioner consensus), **C4 contested/uncertain**. Named modifiers, recorded: down for vested interest, staleness, indirectness, out-of-scope; up for unambiguous mechanism (CVE, mathematical argument, reproducible benchmark) or survival across independent ecosystems.

**The BCP-14 gate** (admission floors, per claim strength). First, a claim-type split the arithmetic needs: **conformance claims** — those that cite a norm *as* the norm ("conform to RFC 9110"; "behaves as the vendor documents") — are satisfied by the norm itself, pinned; for claims about a product's own behaviour, that product's authoritative documentation is **S1-equivalent within that scope** (the scoped-bindingness rule). Corroboration is a test for **empirical claims**, where the source could simply be wrong; it is not a demand for two copies of a norm. Then: **MUST/MUST NOT** ⇒ C1, or C2 plus an explicitly stated risk asymmetry (GRADE's strong-recommendation-on-moderate-evidence move), with conformance MUSTs satisfied by their pinned norm; empirical claims about humans/organisations cannot exceed C2 on practitioner sources alone. **SHOULD** ⇒ ≥C2, single-source flagged visibly. **MAY/illustrative** ⇒ ≥S6, pinned and dated. **X** ⇒ never normative. Where a normative claim genuinely needs sub-floor evidence (the only description of a proprietary mechanism), it enters via a **downref-style exception register** whose entries are [normative-language-applicability-and-exceptions.md](../patterns/normative-language-applicability-and-exceptions.md) §5 exceptions in record shape — claim, source, justification, approver, date, **expiry**: adjudicated once and **reusable while in force** by later claims citing the same source at the same level (the RFC 8067 economics); permanence is a change to policy or profile, never an endlessly renewed exception. The register doubles as the worklist for evidence upgrades.

**Transcription/intermediary rule** (hard case the ledger below exercises): retrieval of the **verbatim artifact** from any host keeps the underlying class with the access path annotated; **re-keyed or transcribed content** keeps the underlying class only when corroborated across ≥2 independent transcriptions; a single uncorroborated transcription takes the **intermediary's** class.

**Citation metadata (absorbing the temporal axis):** `class`, `scope`, `role`, a durable identifier where one exists (DOI, RFC number, dated TR URI — the persistent-identifier layer [M12]) preferred as the canonical leg, `pinned-revision` **or** `rolling-rationale` (rolling only under the ISO conditions: whole-document reference whose *any* future change the claim survives), `accessed-date`, `archived-url` (captured at admission — after admission is too late), optional `content-hash` (drift detection), `publisher-status`, `successor`, `deviation` (the NIST×DoD divergence record: pinned revision + publisher status + owner + expiry), `supports-claims` with strengths, `review-trigger`.

**Known price of this design** (recorded so adoption is eyes-open): the two-axis scheme is the one with the best-documented evidence that raters quietly defeat it (the 87% diagonal) and per-claim grading is expensive to do reliably (GRADE inter-rater κ≈0.27 in early testing [F7]) — it pays off only with a written independence test and periodic **diagonal-collapse audits** (suspiciously many S1/C1, S6/C3 pairs = the axes have collapsed). If the corpus's typed-MUST density were low, a single honest axis plus a mandatory-corroboration rule for MUSTs would be cheaper; at the corpus's actual density — **94 MUST/MUST NOT and 43 SHOULD/SHOULD NOT keywords** across principles, patterns, and ENGINEERING.md (measured 2026-08-12), before the V41 typing retrofit adds more — the two-axis form is justified.

## 6. Lifecycle intersection — additions, promotions, demotions, updates

Keyed to the existing machinery: the change harness, [doctrine-content-lifecycle.md](../patterns/doctrine-content-lifecycle.md) (statuses, sweeps, reflexive house law), [normative-language-applicability-and-exceptions.md](../patterns/normative-language-applicability-and-exceptions.md) (claim anatomy, exception contract), ADR 0026 (revision pinning).

1. **Additions.** The §5 gate runs at admission. Every web citation gets `accessed-date` + `archived-url` at admission (the ~27%-snapshot finding is the argument: archive-at-citation or accept unrecoverable evidence). Sub-element citations (control IDs, section numbers) are always pinned. No normative claim cites a source a future maintainer cannot lawfully obtain.
2. **Promotions (informative → normative) are re-admission at the higher floor, not a status flip:** re-fetch and re-verify every supporting source (content-drift check, refreshed archive), re-grade class, **convert rolling → pinned**, check publisher status (superseded ⇒ re-point to successor or record an explicit deviation with owner and expiry), and call out any sub-floor exceptions in the promotion review — the Last-Call analog. This extends lifecycle §2's evidence expectation with the source-authority dimension it lacks.
3. **Demotions/retirement — source events open reviews anchored to the sweep engines**, not to calendar intent (the lifecycle's own clock law: "clocks are anchored to release tags, never calendar intent"): **rescinded/disavowed sources are immediate on discovery** — supported normative claims are treated as unsupported at once; events on MUST-supporting sources are handled by the **next sweep at latest, prioritised ahead of cohort work**; SHOULD/MAY events join the normal sweep cohort. An estate wanting sub-sweep latency MUST name the monitoring tooling and its owner in the adopting ADR — a calendar SLA without a detection surface is a dead letter (the note's own pass-2 fetch log is the demonstration that automated source-watching is not free). The events: publisher withdrawal/rescission; supersession (re-pin after diff review, or keep the old pin as a registered deviation — never silently); amendment of a pinned source (the ISO "shall be reviewed" duty); link death (swap to successor or archive — archive-only support caps below MUST); content drift (re-verify, re-quote, or re-source); vendor product sunset (re-scope or retire product-scoped claims); access change (public → paywalled re-checks the retrievability gate). Frozen history: a Superseded doctrine file keeps its pinned citations untouched as historical record — supersession points forward via the tombstone, never rewrites backward.
4. **Composition boundaries** (settling what §2 only asserted as precedent): (a) **ADR 0026 takes precedence for profile-carried sources** — where a cited document is also a pinned control-profile baseline (a NIST SP under [revision-pinned-control-profiles.md](../patterns/revision-pinned-control-profiles.md)), supersession fires the profile pattern's own migration machinery, and the citation record points at the profile entry rather than opening a second pin; the §6.3 event table governs ordinary citations only. The §5 `deviation` field generalises the profile pattern's divergence handling to non-profile citations; it does not replace it. (b) **Exception-register entries are ADR 0028 exceptions**: each carries the normative-language §5 minimum record including expiry; "reusable" means later claims cite a live registered decision, and a deviation that should never expire is a change to policy or profile — exactly as that pattern already rules.
5. **Updates — the dated/undated obligation pair:** default is pinned; a pin without a revision-watch is how a corpus ends up unknowingly citing a withdrawn document, so every pin carries a review trigger on source revision. Rolling references are the exception, admissible only with a recorded rationale attesting the ISO conditions, and create a standing monitoring duty plus claim text written to survive arbitrary change (no quoted numbers, no section IDs). The sweep engines (lifecycle §8) gain a **reference-status leg**: each staleness sweep re-checks `publisher-status` and link health for the cohort's citations — mechanising what pass 2 observed by hand (four degraded governance URLs in one afternoon).

| ID | Source | Class | Verification | Establishes |
